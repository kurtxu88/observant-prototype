How it works

Feedback on your own time.

You give feedback through lightweight, async 1:1s — over email, Telegram, or right inside the product. The company drops in with the occasional question; you reply whenever it suits you. Every reply earns rewards, tracked automatically by the minute. No scheduled calls, no surveys.

·Wherever you already areEmail, Telegram, or in the product — your pick. Conversations come to you.
·Reply whenever it suits youAsync by default. A question lands; you answer in your own time, in a sentence or a paragraph.
·Say how you actually use itNo right answers. What worked, what got in the way, what you wish it did — that's the feedback teams want.
+Every minute countsYour participating minutes are logged automatically, so you're paid fairly for your time.
