In-product feedback SDK — demo

This page loads the real /snippet.js exactly the way a customer's app would. All four surfaces below are live: they post to the ingest endpoint and persist (or soft-succeed when no DB is set).

1 · Unsolicited feedback

The floating Feedback button (bottom-right) is always available. Click it, type a note, send.

2 · AI / output eval

A 👍 / 👎 + optional note is attached to this fake AI output via data-observant-eval.

AI Based on your last 30 days, churn risk is concentrated in trial users who never connected a repo. Three of them asked about pricing in support — worth a nudge.

3 · Exit survey

Fire this on a cancel / downgrade / leave action. Here it's wired to a button.

4 · CSAT

A rare 1–5 check-in. Normally auto-fires on a throttle (≤ once / 30 days); here you can summon it.

Open the Network tab and watch the POST /api/selfserve/in-product calls as you interact. Each carries { type, value, note, url, slug }.

The one line a customer adds to their app:

<script async src="https://www.observanthq.com/snippet.js" data-observant></script>
