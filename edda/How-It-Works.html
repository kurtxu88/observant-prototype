How it works

Five moves, running continuously.

Observant runs the whole loop on its own — meeting each user, watching what they actually do, following up in the moment, and surfacing what you'd never hear otherwise. It repeats for as long as learning mode is on; the longer it runs, the more it knows each user.

01
Onboard

Meet each user on their own line.

A warm first conversation — in your product, a browser companion, or email. Their pick, never a shared channel. Observant learns who they are and what they're here to do.

02
Observe

Watch real behavior, not stated intent.

Light periodic check-ins plus the signals from what people actually do. Questions land in context — tied to a workflow they just finished, not floating in a vacuum.

03
Follow up

Open a 1:1 the moment something's interesting.

A drop-off, a power move, a churn signal — Observant starts a deep-dive while it's still fresh in the user's mind.

behavior-triggered, automatically
04
Stay synced

Your team's questions, threaded in live.

Drop a question to Observant and it weaves into ongoing conversations across everyone you're learning from. Need to hear it yourself? Ask for a live 1:1 and Observant finds the time.

05
Surface

What matters rises to the top.

Patterns surface on their own as conversations add up. Or ask a question across everyone and get a grounded answer — in your users' own words — in minutes.

Where it lives

A private line, wherever your user already is.

Observant doesn't pull people into a separate tool. It opens a one-to-one line inside a surface they already use — you offer the surfaces, each user picks one. Always one-to-one, never a shared channel.

In your product

Native to your own build. You forward user and behavior events to Observant over a webhook — a sign-up, a feature used, an order abandoned. Observant reads the event and opens a 1:1 right inside your UI, in context with what the user just did.

Browser companion

A Chrome extension that pops up the moment it spots something worth asking about — a drop-off, a rage-click, a path repeated three times — while the user is on your site. Best for web apps, no engineering to install.

Email

Quiet, asynchronous check-ins for users who'd rather reply from their inbox. Works for any product, with no integration at all.

The webhook is the native path — Observant lives inside your product, not beside it. One user, one line.

Why users keep talking

What keeps your users showing up.

People share honest, continuous feedback when there's something real in it for them. Observant rewards them in the currency they already care about — your product itself, not a $10 gift card.

Product credits

The most native reward of all. Credits, usage, or account perks inside the product they're already using — so giving feedback pays for itself. Users engaging with your product is the most natural incentive there is.

Early access & perks

A first look at what's shipping next, plus the status, swag, and a direct line to the founders that power users actually want. The people shaping the product get treated like it.

Cash — only to jump-start

No users yet? A cash incentive can bootstrap a recruited panel so you start with someone to learn from. It's the on-ramp for cold-start builders — not what the program runs on once your own users are talking.

You set the rewards; Observant handles who earns what, and when.
