How it works

What happens after you turn it on.

Once learning mode is on, Subspace runs the whole loop on its own — meeting each user, watching what they actually do, following up in the moment, and surfacing what you'd otherwise never hear. Here's the whole thing, step by step.

The loop

Five moves, running continuously.

Repeating for as long as learning mode is on. The longer it runs, the more Subspace knows each user.

01
Onboard

Meet each user on their own line.

A warm first conversation — in your product, a browser companion, or email. Their pick, never a shared channel. Subspace learns who they are and what they're here to do.

02
Observe

Watch real behavior, not stated intent.

Light periodic check-ins plus the signals from what people actually do. Questions land in context — tied to a workflow they just finished, not floating in a vacuum.

03
Follow up

Open a 1:1 the moment something's interesting.

A drop-off, a power move, a churn signal — Subspace starts a deep-dive while it's still fresh in the user's mind.

behavior-triggered, automatically
04
Stay synced

Your team's questions, threaded in live.

Drop a question to Subspace and it weaves into ongoing conversations across everyone you're learning from. Need to hear it yourself? Ask for a live 1:1 and Subspace finds the time.

05
Surface

What matters rises to the top.

Patterns surface on their own as conversations add up. Or ask a question across everyone and get a grounded answer — in your users' own words — in minutes.

Where it lives

A private line, wherever your user already is.
