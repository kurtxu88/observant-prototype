The loop

Backed by a diary study, run continuously.

Five moves, repeating for as long as the program runs. The longer it runs, the more Edda knows each user.

01
Onboard

Meet each user on their own line.

A warm first conversation — in your product, a browser companion, email, or the Edda app. Their pick, never a shared channel. Edda learns who they are and what they're here to do.

02
Observe

Watch real behavior, not stated intent.

Light periodic check-ins plus the signals from what people actually do. Questions land in context — tied to a workflow they just finished, not floating in a vacuum.

03
Follow up

Open a 1:1 the moment something's interesting.

A drop-off, a power move, a churn signal — Edda starts a deep-dive while it's still fresh in the user's mind.

behavior-triggered, automatically
04
Stay synced

Your team's questions, threaded in live.

Relay a question through Edda and it weaves into ongoing conversations across the panel. Need to hear it yourself? Request a live 1:1 and Edda finds the time.

05
Synthesize

Insight rises bottom-up — or on demand.

Themes surface on their own as conversations accumulate. Or ask a question across any cohort, behavior, or demographic and get a grounded synthesis in minutes.
